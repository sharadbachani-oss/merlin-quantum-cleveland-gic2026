hardware and killing the classical continuation. Cleveland's best native
card is real. It is not that crossing. This exhibit says exactly what it is.

**Leg 1 — regime.** On the 11-cell KRAS card, U/kT = 106.64. Connected
correlators of a strongly coupled network are not a perturbative object.
That is why the industrial question (distant catalytic control) is mapped
onto a quench, not onto a pocket-ML score.

**Leg 2 — checkable width.** The same instrument is validated where a
classical prediction exists:

- 11q all-to-all: C(1-0) in band vs exact statevector.
- 156q K=2: C(6-7) in band vs an exact 6-qubit lightcone.

**Leg 3 — unverified front.** K=6 on the same 156q assignment. A weight-2
observable's lightcone at this depth is 99 qubits (2⁹⁹). No cone prediction
was registered. 120 pairs were pre-registered; the headline pair at d=11
is 8.68σ from zero.
